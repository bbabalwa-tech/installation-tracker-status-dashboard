Field to Funder

A site installation reporting system for a national water programme: a phone app for the field team and a live dashboard for funders, joined by one Google Sheet.

Portfolio demo by Babalwa Bam. All names, photos and results are fictional demo data. No real participant information.
For funders

Live funder dashboard

Every site, its status, its water quality result and its evidence (photos, walkthrough video, lab documents) in one link. It reads straight from the demo Google Sheet, so new submissions appear after a refresh.

Open the dashboard
For the technical team

Technician phone app

Used on site. The technician picks the site, takes up to three photos, records a short video, adds the lab documents and submits. The app files everything in Drive with the right names and updates the Sheet.

Sign in needs a demo passcode, available on request. Works on Android and iPhone and can be added to the home screen.

Open the phone app

How the two fit together

  1. A technician submits a site from their phone, even on weak signal.
  2. The app saves the photos, video and documents to Google Drive and adds the site to the Google Sheet.
  3. A person checks the lab result and records Pass or Fail. The app never guesses it.
  4. The funder refreshes the dashboard and sees the new site. Nobody rebuilds or resends a file.
